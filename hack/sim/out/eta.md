# ETA bands and suggested deadlines

Generated by `go run ./hack/sim` (the ETA experiment, 5m54s). Main farm, objective policy with burst, seeds [1 2 3 4 5], the same weeks as results.md. Synthetic workload.

The calibration arm's W percentiles come from `estimate.Calibration` built from a separate week (seed 0xe7a) run the same way, per job kind, as the queue builds them live from finished jobs.

## 1. Does the shown band cover the actual finish?

A job's band is recorded when it is first planned, at every binary-order information point (1, 3, 5, 9, 17, 33 units finished) and every hour, and compared with when the job actually finished. P50 to P95 are `forecast.Display`, what `status.estimate.eta50`..`eta95` show; "sched ETA50/90" are the scheduler's own finishes, which decide but are not shown. Error is the median of |P50 - finish| over the time that was left.

## 2. Suggested deadlines against guessed ones

Soft jobs are previews (the workload gives them 3 hours) and ML runs (36 hours). Each arm changes only how their deadlines are set; dailies (08:00), deliveries (Friday 18:00) and CI (15-minute SLO) keep theirs.

- "Suggested at q" and "generous at q" are the farm's suggestion at submission: the earliest deadline the job meets at quantile q of its shown band without making any job that is on time now late (`forecast.Policy` achievable, rounded up to 15 minutes, at least 15 minutes out). The shown band stops at P95, so q = 0.98 extrapolates the band's tail past P95 from P90 and P95 as a normal tail in log time-left; that tail is assumed, not measured.
- "Set once" never moves it (`MaxRevisions` 0). "Moved later only when at risk" is `forecast.Suggestion` with `MaxRevisions` 2, as the queue runs it: at an information point (1, 3, 5, 9, 17, 33 units finished), if the band shown under the current deadline puts P95 after the deadline, the suggestion is searched again and moves to it when that is later by more than max(a tenth of the time left, 15 minutes); never earlier, and settled after two moves, 33 units, or a quarter of the time to the deadline ("at-risk moves").
- The earlier rule, revising up to twice either way when the band narrowed by a fifth, is no longer in `forecast`; its five-seed result on the main workload was 79% of previews on time at a median padding of 1.71 (commit a219938).
- "User tightens to the next session" models a person: right after the 0.95 suggestion, the deadline moves once to the earliest dailies session (08:00 on a weekday) at or after the band's P80 (80% on-time odds), if that is earlier than the suggestion ("tightened"); otherwise the suggestion stands.

Missed and late hours are against each job's own deadline in that arm; turnaround is submission to finish; padding is deadline minus finish for jobs that made it, over their run time (submission to finish). Missed, late hours and burst are means per week over the seeds; on time and medians pool the seeds. Moves and policy runs (the policy runs spent finding the suggestion and checking it) are per suggestion of that kind.

## 3. The same arms when previews compete with deadline work

A workload variant, `contention`, run the same way: its own calibration week (the same seed, the variant's parameters), then seeds [1 2 3 4 5]. It differs from the main workload only in:

- PreviewPerHour: map[showA:3 showB:2.25 showC:1.5] (main workload map[showA:2 showB:1.5 showC:1])
- PreviewCost: 1 (main workload 0.35)
- evening previews per hour, Mon to Fri 17:00 to 22:00: map[showA:1.5 showB:1 showC:0.75] (main workload none)

Previews per week: 350 (main workload 186), each about three times the work. Evening previews are submitted while dailies are (dailies arrive around 19:00, due 08:00), so a preview's deadline decides whether it runs ahead of a daily. Columns as in section 2.
