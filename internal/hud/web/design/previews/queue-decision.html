Decision card

A burst over budget waits for a person (or an agent acting for one). The card says what the slots change (the late finish and the deliveries that slip with it), the project's burst budget today with the price on top of it, and the entry approving it writes to the book: the HUD's seal, dashed until signed, naming who signs. An agent gets the same seal, named. On the desk it is one strip above the shots; on a phone it leads the screen. Below it, the shots band: each shot one aggregate, late shots in a dashed red outline, deliveries named.
