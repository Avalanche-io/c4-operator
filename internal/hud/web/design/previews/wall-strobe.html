Clocks, strobe, ticker

Mission clocks count down in the farm's clock: the deliveries, the jobs at the root of a lateness (with what closes the gap), the next deadline on a shot, the production window as a mission day, and the farm working now. Red when late; a passed deadline counts up. The strobe strip is the most urgent binary-order render as film: frames lit where rendered, blue running, orange retrying, red failed, under a dyadic ruler whose bright ticks show how fine the shot exists now; a frame that just landed glows and settles over a minute. The book runs along the bottom, each entry with its signer's seal.
