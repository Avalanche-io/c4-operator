The C4 ID mark

A C4 ID is 90 characters; shown raw it is noise. A small mark stands in for it: c4 in the corner brackets the callouts lock on with (the maintainer's pick, F). Hover or focus it to read the ID, click it or press Enter to copy it. Where people check that IDs match, the expanded mark adds one to three glyphs drawn from the ID's digest (the maintainer's pick, the 5 × 5 mirrored grid). It all comes from the c4-mark package (), which the HUD vendors.

Ink only. An ID is verification metadata, not a state, so the mark never takes a state hue (design/README.md). It takes the text's ink and brightens on hover and focus; the package's colors are custom properties the HUD sets to its tokens.
