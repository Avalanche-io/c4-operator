The sheet

Every screen is a sheet in a series. A neatline bounds the data; the collar outside it says what, when and at what scale (the title block: deadline in ink, the ETA band and slack in pencil, done by work, slots, the output view as a grid reference). At each edge, the neighbouring sheets are named: upstream jobs to the west, downstream to the east, sibling shots north and south. The world is always larger than the sheet in front of you.
