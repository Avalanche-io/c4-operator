Ledger marks

Nothing on the farm is overwritten. Every action is an entry in the book, signed by whoever acted: a node, the queue, the controller, an importer, or the HUD on behalf of a person or an agent (the same seal, named). A seal is dashed until its record is signed; a superseded entry is struck through, never erased. The edge code carries a job's output view and counts the changesets folded into its chain.
