Dispatch rows

The farm sheet in dispatch order: each job's PYLON (mini), its name and class, its shot, its batches collapsed to aggregates (green done from the bottom, a blue sliver running, orange and red ticks, a dashed red rule under waiting work projected late), its deadline in ink and p90 in pencil, and its slack against the datum. The datum is sea level: one rule down the whole list; height above it in grey pencil, depth below it in red, dashed while projected. A pending burst is a grey dashed button with its price.

batch strip
batches of 24 frames for render, otherwise whatever keeps the strip under forty cells; by units, from unitStates alone, so a row costs one small SVG
the datum
log scale, −6h to +3d, zero at a third of the column; red ink once the deadline has passed
