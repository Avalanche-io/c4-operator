Unit states

One box per unit. Green fills it from the bottom as it progresses: solid when the unit reports progress itself, striped when the fill is elapsed time over the estimate, so an estimate never passes for a measurement. A running unit has a blue body and a breathing blue leading edge. Earlier failed attempts are orange notches. Rows thinner than 6 px fill sideways, because stacked one-pixel lines of blue, green and orange blend into false colors. Below: the same states as collapsed aggregate blocks.
